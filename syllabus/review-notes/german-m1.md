# German Language, Milestone 1: syllabus gaps found in topic reviews

## Gaps logged by the content review of ger.sound-system (2026-10-11)

- **"Spelling-to-sound rules" (§1.1) has no ch clause.** Its line reads "ei vs ie, eu/äu, sch, sp and st at the start of a word, z, v and w; ß after long vowels and diphthongs vs ss after short vowels; every noun capitalised." Two ch rules fall between "The German sound system" (which teaches ich-Laut vs ach-Laut and -chen) and that line:
  - **chs inside a stem is [ks]** (sechs, Fuchs, Dachs, wachsen, Ochse), against [çs]/[xs] across a morpheme boundary (Dachs, genitive of Dach). sechs is an A1 number.
  - **Word-initial ch in loanwords:** [k] before a, o, l, r (Chor, Christ) in every standard; normally [ç] before e, i, y (Chemie), but [k] in southern Germany and Austria.
- **Handling.** "The German sound system" now states both rules minimally (one clause in the ch step, one paragraph in the Deep dive). Suggested syllabus change: add "ch in loanwords and chs" to the "Spelling-to-sound rules" line, so that topic can practise them; until then the sound-system Deep dive is their only home.
