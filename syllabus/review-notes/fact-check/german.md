### German examples and grammar statements (expert review, all correct)
- Sounds: Miete/Mitte vowel length; ich-/ach-Laut; vocalised -er; final devoicing (Tag → [ta:k]); glottal stop; Hölle/Höhle and Küche/Kuchen pairs; ß after long vowels and diphthongs, ss after short vowels; stress in ánrufen vs verstéhen; intonation patterns.
- V2 rule; stem-vowel changes geben → gibt, lesen → liest, fahren → fährt; doch as "yes" to a negative question; nicht vs kein.
- Inseparable prefixes be-, emp-, ent-, er-, ge-, miss-, ver-, zer- (the standard list); "Ich rufe dich an".
- Gender cues: -ung, -heit, -keit, -schaft, -ion → feminine; -chen, -lein → neuter. In the accusative only the masculine article changes. Possessives take the same endings as kein.
- halb drei = 2:30; am 3. Mai.
- Perfect tense: sein for a change of place or state, and with sein, bleiben, werden. Participles: ge-…-t / ge-…-en; no ge- with inseparable prefixes or -ieren verbs; angerufen.
- Dative forms (plural den + -n); dative verbs; accusative-only prepositions (durch, für, gegen, ohne, um) and dative-only prepositions (aus, bei, mit, nach, seit, von, zu); the nine two-way prepositions; legen/liegen, stellen/stehen, setzen/sitzen, hängen; object order (Ich gebe es ihm).
- Adjective-ending principle (strong, weak, mixed); gut/besser/am besten, viel/mehr, gern/lieber; als vs wie.
- Position-0 conjunctions; verb-final subordinate clauses; "Weil es regnet, bleibe ich zu Hause"; Ich wasche mich / mir die Hände.
- fahren–fuhr–gefahren, gehen–ging–gegangen; nachdem + pluperfect; "Er wird krank sein" (assumption); als vs wenn.
- Genitive prepositions wegen, trotz, während, statt. N-declension: Kollege, Student, Mensch, Name/des Namens. Relative pronouns differ from the article only in dessen, deren, denen.
- Konjunktiv II forms; "Wenn ich das gewusst hätte"; double infinitive "Ich hätte es machen müssen".
- zu-infinitive (anzurufen; no zu after modals, lassen, sehen, hören); um/ohne/statt … zu vs damit; warten auf + Akk, sich erinnern an + Akk, teilnehmen an + Dat; darauf/worauf.
- Passive "ist … worden", von/durch; state vs process passive; passive alternatives (sich lassen, sein + zu, -bar: lösbar).
- Middle-field ordering (TeKaMoLo tendency); two-part connectors; extended participial attributes.
- Konjunktiv I (er sei/habe/könne), with Konjunktiv II used where Konjunktiv I matches the indicative; subjective modals soll, muss, will … gesehen haben.
- das Meeting, der Bug, die Daten (plural).
- Arbeitszeugnis: "stets zu unserer vollsten Zufriedenheit" = very good (top grade).
- Arbeit + s + Platz; formal prepositions and their cases; bekommen/erhalten/kriegen; als ob + Konjunktiv II.
- isses (ist es).
- Idioms: auf dem Holzweg sein, ins Fettnäpfchen treten. Terms: Kündigungsfrist, Probezeit, AGB.

## Corrected (old → new, why/source)
1. Ch. 21, Fast speech: "haben wir → hamma" → "haben wir → hamwa, in the south hamma". Why: "hamma" is a southern (Bavarian/Austrian) form; general colloquial and northern speech has "hamwa"/"ham wa". Source: expert knowledge of colloquial German.
2. Appendix: "TestDaF and DSH, both at C1-equivalent level." → "TestDaF and DSH; the usual admission results (TDN 4 in all four parts, DSH-2) are at about C1 level." Why: TestDaF spans B2–C1 (TDN 3–5), and DSH-3 is above the admission level. Only the usual admission thresholds correspond to about C1. Sources: Goethe-Institut TestDaF pages ("TDN 3, 4 und 5 liegen auf den Niveaus B2 bis C1"); HRK/KMK RO-DT; university admission sheets (FAU, Würzburg "4×4 TDN (=DSH-2)").

## Moved
None. Level placements checked against the Goethe-Institut A1 grammar inventory (Fit in Deutsch 1 Wortliste: A1 has the present tense, the Präteritum of haben/sein, the imperative du/ihr, modals, separable verbs, prepositions + dative, and comparison, but no Perfekt) and against usual Profile deutsch / textbook progression. Nothing was clearly wrong. Flagged but not moved:
- Imperative (A2, Ch. 8): the Goethe A1 inventory already has the du/ihr imperative. An A2 treatment that adds Sie and softening is defensible.
- Dative (A2, Ch. 6): A1 inventories have prepositions + dative. A systematic A2 treatment is standard.
- Passive (B2, Ch. 15): many B1 syllabi introduce Passiv Präsens. Treating all tenses and modals at B2 is defensible.
- Konjunktiv II past (B1, Ch. 12): often placed at B2 in textbooks, but B1 also occurs.
