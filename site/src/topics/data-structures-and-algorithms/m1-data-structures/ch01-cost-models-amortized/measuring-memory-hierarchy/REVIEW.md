## 2. Unsupported numbers

- **Real numeric error: narration says "about one and a tenth per append," but the topic's own written pages say ≈1.05.** Narration step 9 (0-indexed), in the "where copies and the clock disagree" reconciliation: "For a million appends that double, we counted just over a million copies in total. **About one and a tenth per append.**" "One and a tenth" means 1.1. But the actual figure — stated three separate times elsewhere in this same topic — is **1,048,575 / 1,000,000 ≈ 1.05**: `index.mdx`'s internals section ("≈ 1.05 copies per append, not 2"), `index.mdx`'s Part 2 of the lab ("≈ 1.05 per append"), and `prerequisites.mdx`'s own check-yourself ("about 1.05 per append"). 1.1 overstates the fractional excess over 1 by roughly double (0.1 vs. the true 0.0486). Since this is exactly the number the lab's part 2 and part 4 exist to reconcile, getting it wrong in the one place a learner hears it spoken is worth fixing — e.g. "just over one, about one and a twentieth" or simply "about one point oh five."
- All other numbers check out: the canonical 1,048,575-copies-over-20-resizes figure (matching `CHAPTER.md`'s running-example table exactly); the "512×" huge-page-reach multiplier (2 MiB / 4 KiB = 512, recomputed and correct); the 64-byte line holding 8 sixty-four-bit keys (64/8 = 8, correct); the DRAM/L1 "hundredfold" latency ratio (100 ns / ~1 ns = 100, correct); and the "~34" resizes for a million appends at g = 1.5 is explicitly hedged with "~" rather than asserted as exact (log₁.₅(10⁶) ≈ 34.1, a reasonable approximation, unlike the g = 2 figure which the chapter fixes exactly).
