- "Parametric search" (Wikipedia) — the technique's name, year and Megiddo's own attribution and stated caveat. [source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Parametric_search)
- "Binary Search" (cp-algorithms.com) — the generalisation to an arbitrary monotone predicate and to a real-valued continuous search. [source](https://cp-algorithms.com/num_methods/binary_search.html)

Megiddo's own paper ("Applying Parallel Computation Algorithms in the Design of Serial Algorithms", *Journal of the ACM* 30(4), 852–865, 1983) sits behind the ACM Digital Library's paywall and was not opened this session; every claim about it here is sourced through Wikipedia's own quoted summary, recorded as such. Every numeric claim this topic makes (the capacity search's answer, the bisection iteration counts) is computed and asserted directly in `examples.py`.
