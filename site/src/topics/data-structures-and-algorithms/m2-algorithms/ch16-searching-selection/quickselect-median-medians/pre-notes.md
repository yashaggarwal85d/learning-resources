- **Order statistic** (one line): the k-th smallest element of a collection under a total order; the median is the order statistic at the middle rank. *Where you meet it:* what quickselect and median-of-medians both compute, without sorting the rest of the collection.
- **Partition** (recap): rearranging an array around a chosen pivot so everything no larger than it ends up on one side and everything no smaller ends up on the other. *Where you meet it:* the one step from Chapter 15 this topic reuses without rebuilding.
- **Expected value versus worst case** (recap): the average outcome over all random choices an algorithm makes, versus the single worst outcome any input could force. *Where you meet it:* quickselect's expected linear time against median-of-medians' guaranteed linear time.
- **Recursion** (recap): a function that calls a smaller version of itself, with a base case that stops it. *Where you meet it:* median-of-medians recursing on the list of group-medians before using the result as a pivot.
