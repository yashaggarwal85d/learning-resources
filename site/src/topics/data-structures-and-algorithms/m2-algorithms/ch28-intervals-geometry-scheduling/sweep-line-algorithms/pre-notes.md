- **Segment**: a straight line piece between two points.
- **Axis-parallel rectangle**: a rectangle whose sides are parallel to the x- and y-axes.
- **Intersection point**: the single point where two line segments cross.
- **Cross product sign**: for two vectors, positive means a counter-clockwise turn from the
  first to the second; it tells you which side of a line a point falls on.
- **Balanced search tree**: an ordered structure supporting insert, delete, and
  predecessor/successor queries in O(log n).
- **Priority queue**: a structure that always gives you the smallest (or largest) item next.
- **General position**: no three segments pass through one point, no segment is vertical, no
  two segments share an endpoint — the simplifying assumption this topic works under.
- **Output-sensitive**: a running-time bound that depends on the size of the *answer*, not
  just the size of the input.
