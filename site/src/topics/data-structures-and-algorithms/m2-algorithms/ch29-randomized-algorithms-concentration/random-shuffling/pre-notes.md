- **Permutation**: an ordering of n distinct items; there are n! of them.
- **Factorial**: n! = n·(n − 1)·…·1, the count of distinct orderings of n items.
- **Cyclic permutation**: one cycle through every item, so no item ever stays in its own place.
- **Uniformly random choice**: every available option equally likely to be picked.
- **Pseudo-random generator**: a deterministic sequence produced from a seed; the number of
  distinct internal states it can hold bounds how many different outputs it can ever produce.
- **Chi-squared test**: compares a table of observed counts to the counts a hypothesis
  predicts; a large statistic means the observed counts are unlikely under that hypothesis.
- **Pointer chasing**: following `p = next[p]` one step at a time, each step depending on the
  last (from Chapter 1's memory-hierarchy benchmark).
