- **Polynomial time against exponential time**: a cost bounded by a fixed power of the input's
  length, against a cost that grows like a constant raised to a power of that length.
- **Bit length of a number**: how many binary digits it takes to write the number down, roughly
  the logarithm (base two) of its value.
- **Binary against unary encoding**: a number written in positional digits, against that same
  number written as that many tally marks in a row.
- **Dynamic-programming table**: a grid with one cell per subproblem, filled once and reused.
- **The knapsack problem**: choosing items under a weight limit to maximize value.
- **The bin packing problem**: packing items of different sizes into the fewest bins of one
  fixed capacity.
- **NP-complete**: already defined in the previous topic; a decision problem that is both
  NP-hard and itself in NP.
