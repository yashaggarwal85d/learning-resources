- **Traveling salesman problem**: find the shortest closed tour that visits every point exactly once and returns to the start. *Where you meet it:* the one problem local search is taught on in this topic, and the one PostgreSQL's own genetic planner is modeled on.
- **Neighborhood**: the set of solutions reachable from the current one by a single small change. *Where you meet it:* two-opt's neighborhood is every tour reachable by reversing one segment.
- **Local optimum**: a solution with no better neighbor, even though a better solution may exist elsewhere. *Where you meet it:* what every search in this topic can get stuck at.
- **Temperature (as a control parameter)**: a number that starts high and falls over time, controlling how willing a search is to accept a worse move. *Where you meet it:* simulated annealing's own acceptance rule.
- **Population, crossover**: a genetic algorithm keeps many candidate solutions at once (a population) and builds new ones by combining pieces of two existing ones (crossover). *Where you meet it:* both the topic's own genetic algorithm and PostgreSQL's real GEQO.
- **Join order**: the sequence in which a database query's tables are combined pairwise. *Where you meet it:* the problem PostgreSQL's genetic query optimizer actually solves, encoded as a string the same way a tour is.
- **Cost estimate**: a query planner's own prediction of how expensive a plan will be to run, used to compare candidate plans before any of them actually runs. *Where you meet it:* the number a genetic query optimizer is trying to minimize, standing in for the real, unknown cost.
