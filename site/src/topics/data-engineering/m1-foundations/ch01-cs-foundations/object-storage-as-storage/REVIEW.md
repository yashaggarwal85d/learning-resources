## 2. Unsupported numbers

- **One real numeric/unit slip: "a minimum part size of about 110 MiB."** Internals, "Multipart upload": "the part count is bounded: 1 TiB over at most 10,000 parts means a minimum part size of about 110 MiB." Computed precisely: 1 TiB = 1,048,576 MiB ÷ 10,000 parts = **104.86 MiB** (binary units, matching the "5 MiB to 5 GiB" limit's own units) — not 110. The "110" appears to come from dividing the *decimal*-MB wire-byte figure computed two paragraphs earlier (≈1,099,512 MB) by 10,000 = 109.95 ≈ 110, but that number is in MB (decimal), not MiB (binary), and the draft's own table a few lines above uses MiB for the part-size limit. Either say "≈105 MiB" (consistent with the limit's own units) or "≈110 MB" (consistent with the decimal figure it was derived from) — not "110 MiB," which mixes the two. Doesn't change the point (the forced minimum is well above the "switch to multipart around 100 MB" threshold either way).
- All other numbers — the α·β ≈ 9 MB break-even, the ≈3.4-hour single-stream transfer time for 1 TiB, the ≈14-concurrent-request estimate to fill a 10 Gbit/s link, "a tenth of the bytes" for 3 of 30 columns — were independently recomputed and are correct.
