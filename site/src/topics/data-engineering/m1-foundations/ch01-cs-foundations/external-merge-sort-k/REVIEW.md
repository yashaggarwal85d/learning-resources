## 2. Unsupported numbers

None found. Every arithmetic claim was independently recomputed and is correct:
- 1 TiB ÷ 16 GiB = **64 runs** (1,024 GiB ÷ 16 GiB).
- Fan-in = M/B − 1 = 16,384 − 1 = **16,383**.
- Two-at-a-time merging of 64 runs needs ⌈log₂ 64⌉ = **6 merge passes** (7 total) — correct.
- The "16,384 runs → 2 merge passes, 3 total" and "up to 16,383 runs → 1 merge pass, 2 total" rows of the pass-count table are both correct given `passes = 1 + ⌈log_fan-in(runs)⌉`.
