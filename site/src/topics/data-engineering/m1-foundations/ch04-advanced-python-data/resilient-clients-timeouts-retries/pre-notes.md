- **Deadline propagation**: sharing one absolute deadline down a call chain, rather than each hop inventing its own fresh timeout. *Where you meet it:* the deadline-propagation reveal, and the "Prove it" scenario's second failure.
- **Jitter**: deliberate randomness added to a backoff delay, specifically to stop clients that failed together from retrying together. *Where you meet it:* the predict step and its reveal, the center of this topic's biggest counter-intuitive result.
- **Retry budget**: a cap on how many retries a client is allowed to spend in total, independent of any single call's own backoff schedule. *Where you meet it:* the retry-budget reveal, contrasted directly against the circuit breaker.
- **Circuit breaker**: a wrapper around a call that stops calling a dependency at all once it looks unhealthy, instead of retrying it into the ground. *Where you meet it:* the circuit-breaker reveal's closed/open/half-open states.
- **Idempotency key**: a value a client attaches to a write so a server can recognize and safely replay a retried request instead of repeating its effect. *Where you meet it:* the idempotency reveal and the "Prove it" scenario's first failure.
