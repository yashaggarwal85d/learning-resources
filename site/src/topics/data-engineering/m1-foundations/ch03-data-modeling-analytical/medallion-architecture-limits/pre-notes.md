- **Medallion architecture**: organizing lakehouse data into Bronze, Silver, and Gold layers, each progressively improving quality and structure. *Where you meet it:* the layering convention this whole topic is built around.
- **Layer sprawl**: forcing data through more transformation stages than it genuinely needs, paying real latency and storage cost for no matching benefit. *Where you meet it:* the first named failure mode, caused by applying the convention where it doesn't actually fit.
- **Fragmented accountability**: no single team clearly owning data-quality correctness, because different teams transform the data at different stages. *Where you meet it:* why a bug discovered in Gold isn't necessarily Gold's own team's problem to fix.
- **Enterprise view**: a conformed, "just-enough" cleansed representation of an organization's key business entities, not yet shaped for any one project. *Where you meet it:* the Silver layer's own stated contract, and the reason it ends up serving — and bottlenecking — every downstream consumer at once.
