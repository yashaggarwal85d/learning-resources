- **Domain ownership**: each business domain hosts and serves its own analytical data, instead of a central team extracting it into a shared lake. *Where you meet it:* the first principle, reversing the direction data has flowed through this whole chapter so far.
- **Data as a product**: domain data treated like a real product, with its own named standard — discoverable, addressable, trustworthy, inter-operable — not just "data someone made available." *Where you meet it:* the second principle, and the specific checklist this topic quotes directly.
- **Self-serve platform**: shared infrastructure that removes the specialized skill every domain would otherwise need to build and run its own pipeline from scratch. *Where you meet it:* the principle whose absence is this topic's own named failure mode.
- **Federated computational governance**: domain-local autonomy paired with a small set of global rules, enforced automatically by the platform rather than by a central team's manual review. *Where you meet it:* the fourth principle, closing the loop on the other three.
