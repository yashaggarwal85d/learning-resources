- **Data lake**: low-cost, open-format, schema-on-read storage with a file API, holding raw data outside any one database engine. *Where you meet it:* the second generation, cheap but without a warehouse's transactions or governance by default.
- **Data swamp**: a data lake that lost track of what it holds, because nothing enforced transactions, data quality, or governance on it. *Where you meet it:* the named risk of running a lake without the management features a warehouse used to provide.
- **Open table format**: a transactional layer (Delta Lake, Apache Iceberg) tracked over otherwise ordinary open files in a data lake. *Where you meet it:* what gives a lakehouse its transactions back without handing the files to one owning engine.
- **Schema-on-read**: a storage model where data is written in its raw form and interpreted into a schema only when it's read, rather than enforced at write time. *Where you meet it:* what makes a data lake cheap and flexible to load into, and part of why it needs a transactional layer on top to stay governed.
