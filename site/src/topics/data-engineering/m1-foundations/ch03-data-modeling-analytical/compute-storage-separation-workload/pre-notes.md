- **Virtual warehouse (compute cluster)**: an independent pool of compute resources that doesn't share capacity with any other cluster. *Where you meet it:* the unit that gets assigned per workload, and the unit that's scaled when concurrency rises.
- **Multi-cluster scaling**: adding or removing whole compute clusters, onto the same shared storage, in response to how many queries are running at once. *Where you meet it:* the vendor's own disclosed limit — it helps concurrency, not a single slow query.
- **Noisy neighbor**: one workload's load degrading another unrelated workload's performance because they share the same compute. *Where you meet it:* the problem per-workload cluster isolation exists to remove.
- **Warehouse-local cache**: a compute cluster's own cache of the table data its recent queries have touched, held for as long as that cluster keeps running. *Where you meet it:* a deliberate, partial re-coupling of storage and compute, dropped the moment the cluster is suspended.
