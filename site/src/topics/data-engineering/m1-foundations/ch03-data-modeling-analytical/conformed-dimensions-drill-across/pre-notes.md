- **Conformed dimension**: a dimension table whose attributes share the same column names and domain contents across every fact table that uses it. *Where you meet it:* the same `order_date` dimension, with identical columns, joined to both an orders fact and a shipments fact.
- **Enterprise bus matrix**: the planning grid with business processes as rows and conformed dimensions as columns, marking which dimension each process actually shares. *Where you meet it:* a spreadsheet used in requirements gathering, before any fact table is built.
- **Drill-across**: aggregating two fact tables separately to a shared conformed grain, then aligning the two summarized results by that shared row header, never by a row-level join. *Where you meet it:* a BI report that stitches together sales and support-ticket totals by the same date, without ever joining the two fact tables to each other.
- **Fan-out**: the row-count explosion that happens when two fact tables of different grains are joined directly, rather than drilled across. *Where you meet it:* a report whose total is suddenly several times too large, right after someone "simplified" a drill-across into a single join.
