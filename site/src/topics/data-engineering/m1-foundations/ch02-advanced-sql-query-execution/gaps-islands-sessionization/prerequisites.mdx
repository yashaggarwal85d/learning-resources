### <span class="n">00</span> Basics: words the reel takes for granted

- **Event stream**: a log of timestamped records, one per thing that happened. *Where you meet it:* a clickstream or an application log.
- **Session**: a burst of activity by one user, ended by a period of inactivity. *Where you meet it:* "visits" in a web analytics report.
- **Inactivity threshold**: the pause that ends a session. *Where you meet it:* 30 minutes in many analytics tools.
- **Interval**: a span of time, as a subtraction of two timestamps. *Where you meet it:* `INTERVAL '30 minutes'` in SQL.
- **Run (island)**: an unbroken stretch of consecutive rows meeting a condition. *Where you meet it:* consecutive days a user logged in.

<p class="check"><b>Check yourself:</b> activity on days 1, 2, 3, 5, 6. How many unbroken runs are there? (Answer: two, days 1 to 3 and days 5 to 6.)</p>
