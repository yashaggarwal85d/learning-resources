### <span class="n">00</span> Basics: words the reel takes for granted

- **Tie**: two rows with the same value in the ordering column. *Where you meet it:* two customers with the same order count.
- **Top-N per group**: the first N rows of each group, not of the whole table. *Where you meet it:* the three newest orders of every customer.
- **Primary key**: a column whose values are unique per row. *Where you meet it:* `order_id`.
- **Idempotent job**: a job that gives the same result however many times it runs. *Where you meet it:* a nightly dedup that must not change its answer on a rerun.
- **Logical order of clauses**: the order in which SQL conceptually evaluates `FROM`, `WHERE`, `GROUP BY`, `HAVING`, window functions and `ORDER BY`. *Where you meet it:* why an alias from the select list is not visible in `WHERE`.

<p class="check"><b>Check yourself:</b> why is <code>ORDER BY score DESC</code> alone not a total order if two rows share a score? (Answer: the order of the tied rows is unspecified.)</p>
