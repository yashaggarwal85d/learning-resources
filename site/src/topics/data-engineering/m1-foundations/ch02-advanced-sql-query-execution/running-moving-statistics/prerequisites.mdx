### <span class="n">00</span> Basics: words the reel takes for granted

- **Percentile**: the value below which a given fraction of observations fall. *Where you meet it:* "p99 latency" on a dashboard.
- **Median**: the 50th percentile, the middle value of the sorted data. *Where you meet it:* median income, less distorted by extremes than the mean.
- **Interpolation**: estimating a value between two known ones. *Where you meet it:* the median of an even-sized set is the average of the two middle values.
- **Decile / quartile**: one of ten or four equal-sized slices of ranked data. *Where you meet it:* "top decile of customers by spend".
- **Moving average**: the average of a sliding window of recent values. *Where you meet it:* smoothed charts of daily metrics.

<p class="check"><b>Check yourself:</b> what is the median of 10, 20, 30, 40? (Answer: 25, the average of the two middle values.)</p>
