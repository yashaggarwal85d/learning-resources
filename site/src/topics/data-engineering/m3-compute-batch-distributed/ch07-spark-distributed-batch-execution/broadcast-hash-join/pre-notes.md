- **Build side**: the join input Spark collects and makes available for fast lookup (hashed, for an equi-join; scanned in full, for a nested-loop one). *Where you meet it:* the role the small table plays in every reveal this topic measures.
- **Driver**: the single process that plans the job and, for a broadcast, collects the small side's rows before shipping them out. *Where you meet it:* the process whose own memory this topic's sourced-only half names as the risk behind a stale-statistics broadcast.
- **Equi-join**: a join condition built entirely from equality comparisons (`a.k = b.k`). *Where you meet it:* the exact distinction this topic's third claim turns on — present for a hash join, absent for a nested-loop one.
- **Table statistics**: Spark's own stored estimates of a table's size and row count, used to decide whether a broadcast is safe before the join actually runs. *Where you meet it:* the thing this topic's own danger clause says can go stale and mislead the planner.
