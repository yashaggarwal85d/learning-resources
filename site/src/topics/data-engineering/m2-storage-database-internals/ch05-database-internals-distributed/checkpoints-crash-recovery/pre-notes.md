- **Checkpoint**: an engine's own operation that flushes every dirty buffer to disk and marks a new, more recent starting point for crash recovery, already named in the last topic's full-page-write reveal. *Where you meet it:* every step, measured for real against this sandbox's own cluster.
- **Redo record**: the exact WAL position a checkpoint marks as where recovery should resume replay from. *Where you meet it:* the redo-record reveal, read directly off this sandbox's own `pg_control_checkpoint()`.
- **Write-ahead log (WAL)**: the append-only durability log the last topic established; this topic is entirely about what bounds how far back it must be replayed. *Where you meet it:* throughout, assumed from the last topic.
- **Full-page write**: the last topic's own torn-page defense, logging an entire page on its first change after a checkpoint. *Where you meet it:* the frequency-trade-off reveal and the real measurement steps, reused directly rather than retaught.
- **Transaction**: a unit of change whose own commit status decides whether its effects are visible, already named in earlier chapters. *Where you meet it:* the redo-vs-undo reveal, assumed rather than retaught.
