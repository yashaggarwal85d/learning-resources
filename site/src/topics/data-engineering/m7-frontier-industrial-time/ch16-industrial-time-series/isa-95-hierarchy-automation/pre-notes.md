- **PLC**: a programmable logic controller, the device that directly runs a machine's control logic on the plant floor. *Where you meet it:* named as a level-one/level-two device throughout.
- **Sensor**: a device that measures something physical (temperature, position, a count) and reports a number. *Where you meet it:* the bottom of the hierarchy path this chapter uses everywhere.
- **Dimension table**: a table of descriptive attributes (here, a piece of equipment) that fact rows join to, from Chapter 3. *Where you meet it:* the equipment hierarchy is modelled as one.
- **Surrogate key**: a key generated by the system itself (not a business identifier) that uniquely identifies one row of a table. *Where you meet it:* each hierarchy row, including each of a moved machine's two rows, gets its own.
- **Slowly changing dimension (SCD)**: a dimension whose rows change over time in a tracked way rather than being overwritten in place. *Where you meet it:* the equipment hierarchy, built as a type-2 SCD across a machine's move.
