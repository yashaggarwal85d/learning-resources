## Red team

- **Is the 1297-actual-vs-4320-worst-case ratio realistic, or tuned to produce a dramatic-looking number?** The ratio comes directly from a stated, disclosed rule (each piece of equipment is fitted with a random subset of 2 to 5 of the 5 possible sensor types, plus one status series) applied with a fixed seed — it isn't picked after the fact to hit a target ratio; a real plant's actual fitment pattern would differ in degree but the same mechanism (not every sensor on every piece of equipment) is what CHAPTER.md's own "worst case vs. actual" framing asks this topic to demonstrate.
- **Does omitting `hyperloglog-distinct-counting`/`probabilistic-membership-filters`/`per-series-state-cold-start-churn` understate what this topic depends on?** The postings index and the series-count arithmetic this topic builds are both exact, not probabilistic, so the topic's own executed content doesn't actually need those three ids to function; `OUTLINE.md`/`SOURCES.md` document the gap explicitly rather than silently dropping the "cheap estimate first" framing CHAPTER.md gestures at.
- **Is the regex-matcher cost claim (#9) too weak to be meaningful?** It's phrased as a lower bound deliberately, because a regex matcher's actual cost depends on how many distinct label values it has to scan union across, which varies by query — the weaker, always-true claim (it inspects at least as many series as an equality matcher's single postings list) is the part that's true regardless of which regex is chosen, and is what's asserted.
- **Does the InfluxDB 3 "unlimited cardinality" claim get treated as this topic's own measured result?** No — `SOURCES.md`/`FACT-CHECK.md` both mark it as a vendor claim, sourced-only, and `examples.py`'s own columnar-scan measurement uses DuckDB as a stand-in scan engine to demonstrate the mechanism (no per-series index, an ordinary predicate scan) rather than reproducing InfluxDB 3's own benchmark number.
- **Checked `reel.tsx`, `scenes.tsx`, `narration.json` for leaked exact phrasing from the quoted sources** — none found.
