## Conway — How Do Committees Invent?

URL: http://www.melconway.com/research/committees.html

> How Do Committees Invent?

> Melvin E. Conway

> Copyright 1968, F. D.

> Reprinted by permission of

> Datamation magazine,

> where it appeared April, 1968.

> The basic thesis of this article is that organizations
 which design systems (in the broad sense used here) are
 constrained to produce designs which are copies of the
