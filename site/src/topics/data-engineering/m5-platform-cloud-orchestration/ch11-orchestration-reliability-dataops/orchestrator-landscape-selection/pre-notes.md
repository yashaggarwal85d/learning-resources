# Before you watch

- **Managed service** — a vendor runs the control plane (scheduler, metadata store, HA) for you, in exchange for less self-run flexibility.
- **MWAA / Cloud Composer** — AWS's and Google's own managed Airflow offerings; MWAA Serverless removes even the fixed-environment sizing decision.
- **Prefect** — a Python-native orchestrator with dynamic, event-driven flows, now on its 3.0 line.
- **Kestra** — a YAML-declarative orchestrator: workflows are defined, not coded.
- **State machine** — a serverless workflow defined as explicit states and transitions (AWS Step Functions, Google Workflows), with no cluster to run yourself.
- **Standard vs. Express workflow** — Step Functions' two execution modes, trading duration and delivery guarantees against cost and visibility.
- **Distributed Map** — Step Functions' own managed fan-out primitive for large-scale parallel work.
