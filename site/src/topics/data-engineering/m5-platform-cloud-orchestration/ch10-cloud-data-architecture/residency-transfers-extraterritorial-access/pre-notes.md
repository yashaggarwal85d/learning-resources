# Pre-notes: Residency, transfers and extraterritorial access

- **Residency.** The physical or legal region where data is actually stored.
- **Sovereignty.** Who can be legally compelled to produce data, regardless of where it's stored — the US CLOUD Act's own reach is the canonical example.
- **Localisation.** A law that requires residency as a precondition — certain data must never leave a jurisdiction.
- **Schrems II.** The 2020 CJEU ruling that invalidated the EU-US Privacy Shield framework over US surveillance-law concerns.
- **Standard Contractual Clauses.** The Commission's pre-approved model contract clauses, now requiring a transfer-impact assessment to remain valid.
- **Transfer-impact assessment.** The exporter's evaluation of a destination country's actual surveillance-law exposure before relying on a transfer mechanism.
- **Data Privacy Framework.** The adequacy decision that replaced Privacy Shield, currently upheld once and under appeal.
