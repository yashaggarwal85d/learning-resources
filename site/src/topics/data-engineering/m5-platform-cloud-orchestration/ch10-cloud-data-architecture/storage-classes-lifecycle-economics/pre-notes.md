- **Storage tier**: a named class of object storage with its own price for keeping a byte, its own price and delay for reading it back, and often a minimum time it must be kept. *Where you meet it:* S3 Standard-IA, GCS Nearline, Azure Cool — one of several tiers inside the same bucket.
- **Retrieval charge**: a per-GB fee charged when you read an object back out of a cooler tier, on top of its storage price. *Where you meet it:* a line item that only appears once you actually restore or download an archived file.
- **Lifecycle rule**: a policy that automatically transitions or deletes objects once they match an age, tag or prefix condition, with no human triggering each action. *Where you meet it:* a rule you write once that then runs forever, on a schedule.
- **Snapshot expiry**: a table format's own maintenance operation that drops old snapshots from its metadata, releasing their data files once nothing live still needs them. *Where you meet it:* Iceberg's `expire_snapshots`, Delta's `VACUUM`.
- **Object Lock**: a per-object retention setting that blocks deletion or overwrite until a retention date passes, in governance mode (overridable with a permission) or compliance mode (not overridable by anyone, including the account root). *Where you meet it:* a ransomware or regulatory-retention defense you turn on per bucket or per object.
- **WORM**: write once, read many — the general property Object Lock and its equivalents enforce. *Where you meet it:* the term used across vendors for the same immutability guarantee.
