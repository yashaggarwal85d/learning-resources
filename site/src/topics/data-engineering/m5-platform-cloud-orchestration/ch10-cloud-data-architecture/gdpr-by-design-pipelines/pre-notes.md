# Pre-notes: GDPR by design in pipelines

- **Purpose limitation.** Data collected for one stated reason may not be reused for an incompatible one.
- **Minimisation.** Collecting or storing only the fields a stated purpose actually needs, decided at ingestion rather than after the fact.
- **Storage limitation.** Keeping identifiable data for no longer than its purpose actually requires.
- **Art. 30 record.** A controller's written record of what processing it carries out, on what data, for what purpose and how long.
- **DPIA.** A data protection impact assessment: a required, pre-processing risk assessment for specifically named high-risk categories of processing.
- **Breach notification.** The requirement to tell a supervisory authority about a personal data breach within a fixed window that starts at awareness of the breach.
