### <span class="n">01</span> Before this: segments, and offsets as just another log

This topic reuses the partition-strip model directly and treats a consumer group's offset topic
as one more example of a compacted log, without re-teaching either. If segments or consumer-group
offsets are unfamiliar, start with
[Partitioned logs, segments and indexes](../partitioned-log-segments-indexes/) and
[Consumer groups and rebalance protocols](../consumer-groups-rebalance-protocols/) first.
