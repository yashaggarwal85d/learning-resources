### <span class="n">01</span> Before this: partitioned logs and the controller quorum

This topic treats `__consumer_offsets` as just another compacted partition, and reuses the
controller quorum's own broker-driven, pull-based shape for how a group coordinator pushes
assignments. If segments, offsets, or the controller quorum are unfamiliar, start with
[Partitioned logs, segments and indexes](../partitioned-log-segments-indexes/) and
[KRaft: metadata as a replicated log](../kraft-metadata-replicated-log/) first.
