### <span class="n">01</span> Before this: segments, compaction, and what order means

This topic builds on the segment-rolling model, compaction's own distinct retention rule, and the
partition-ordering vocabulary, without re-teaching any of them. If segments, compaction, or
ordering are unfamiliar, start with
[Partitioned logs, segments and indexes](../partitioned-log-segments-indexes/),
[Retention and log compaction](../retention-log-compaction/), and
