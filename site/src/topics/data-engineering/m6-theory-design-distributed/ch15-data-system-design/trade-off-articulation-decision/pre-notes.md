- **Trade-off**: giving up some property of a design to gain another, made explicit rather than
  left implicit. *Where you meet it:* the three-alternatives reveal.
- **Alternative**: one option in a decision card, priced in the same units as every other option
  considered. *Where you meet it:* the three-alternatives reveal.
- **Decision record (ADR)**: a short, numbered document capturing one architecturally
  significant decision and its rationale. *Where you meet it:* the five-parts reveal.
- **Bottleneck**: the first component that overflows under a stated multiple of load. *Where you
  meet it:* the 10× frame reveal.
- **Whiteboard design**: a timed, structured design session run against the clock. *Where you
  meet it:* the hook and "Prove it."
- **Review**: a peer attacking a finished design document for a stated time, to surface what
  would change. *Where you meet it:* named in the lab section.
