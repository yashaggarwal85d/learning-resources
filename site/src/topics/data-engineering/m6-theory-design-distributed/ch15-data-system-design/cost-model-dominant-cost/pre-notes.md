- **Unit cost**: a design's cost re-expressed per unit of business volume (per thousand orders,
  per million events, per tenant) rather than as a raw dollar total. *Where you meet it:* the
  five-classes reveal.
- **Compute**: instance-hours, serverless units, or scan-billed bytes — one of the five cost
  classes. *Where you meet it:* the five-classes reveal.
- **Storage**: cost by GB-months, varying by storage tier. *Where you meet it:* the five-classes
  reveal.
- **Egress**: cross-AZ, cross-region, and internet data transfer cost. *Where you meet it:* the
  five-classes reveal.
- **Request cost**: object-store and API call charges. *Where you meet it:* the five-classes
  reveal.
- **Licence**: per-seat, per-core, or per-credit software cost. *Where you meet it:* the
  five-classes reveal.
- **Scan volume**: bytes read by a query, billed per byte on scan-priced warehouses — one of the
  three recurring dominators. *Where you meet it:* the hook and the three-dominators reveal.
- **Availability zone**: an isolated location within a cloud region — assumed vocabulary this
  topic doesn't re-derive. *Where you meet it:* named in the three-dominators reveal's framing.
- **Idle capacity**: compute provisioned for peak load that sits unused off-peak. *Where you meet
  it:* the three-dominators reveal.
