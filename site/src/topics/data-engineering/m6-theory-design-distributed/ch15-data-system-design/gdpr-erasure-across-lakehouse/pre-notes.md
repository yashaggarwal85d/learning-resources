- **Data subject**: the person an erasure request is about. *Where you meet it:* the hook and
  the subject-index reveal.
- **Erasure request**: a data subject's exercise of their right to have their personal data
  deleted. *Where you meet it:* the hook.
- **Deadline**: the time limit GDPR sets on acting on an erasure request. *Where you meet it:*
  the delete-expire reveal.
- **Snapshot expiry**: removing an old table-format snapshot so it can no longer be read by time
  travel. *Where you meet it:* the delete-expire reveal.
- **Backup**: a separate stored copy of data, outside the table a delete operation reaches.
  *Where you meet it:* the hook and the blind-spots reveal.
- **Tombstone**: a marker in a log that a key's value has been deleted. *Where you meet it:* the
  hook.
- **Crypto-shredding**: deleting an encryption key so every copy of the data it protects becomes
  unreadable. *Where you meet it:* the crypto-shredding reveal.
- **Proof of deletion**: verifiable evidence that an erasure request was actually carried out.
  *Where you meet it:* the proof reveal.
- **Legal hold**: a rule that keeps data past its normal deletion point for a stated legal
  reason. *Where you meet it:* the predict step and the delete-expire reveal.
